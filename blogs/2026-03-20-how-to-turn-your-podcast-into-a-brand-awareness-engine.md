In the digital gold rush of 2026, attention is the rarest commodity. While social media feeds are scrolled in seconds and blog posts are skimmed in moments, podcasting is the only medium that commands “undivided” time. When a listener hits play, they aren’t just consuming content; they are spending their commute, their workout, or their morning coffee with your brand.
