With more content available online than ever before, it can be easy to assume that music, clips, images, or audio found on the internet are free to use. But in reality, most online content is still protected by copyright. Many podcasters accidentally run into copyright issues by using copyrighted background music, intro tracks, video clips, or images without permission.
